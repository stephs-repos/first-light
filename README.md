# First Light

Twenty-five years of Pacific islands answering climate change with sunlight.

An interactive scrollytelling data visualisation — an entry for the
[Pacific Dataviz Challenge 2026](https://pacificdatavizchallenge.org) (theme: climate change).

**Live: https://stephs-repos.github.io/where-the-day-begins/**

Data: the Affordable and Clean Energy (installed renewable capacity, IRENA),
Power generation, and Climate change (per-capita greenhouse-gas emissions)
dataflows of the [SPC Pacific Data Hub](https://stats.pacificdata.org); world
emissions average from Jones et al. via Our World in Data (CC-BY 4.0).
Backdrops: Digital Earth Pacific GeoMAD (CC-BY 4.0), NASA Blue Marble,
Natural Earth (public domain) — context, not measurement.
Attribution to the Pacific Community (SPC).

This repository hosts the built site only.
